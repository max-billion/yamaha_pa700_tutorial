# Korg Pa700 Live Performance Crash Course

This stage guide summarizes the exact mental model and physical button sequences from the **Korg Pa700 User Manual (OS v1.5)** so you can walk up to the keyboard at your gig and control voices, the Auto-Arranger, and the Split Point with confidence.

---

## 1. Mental Model: "Keyboard Sets" vs. "Sounds"

On a Korg Pa700, you almost never pick raw voices one by one during a song—you pick **Keyboard Sets**:
- A **Sound** is a single instrument patch (e.g., *Concert Grand*, *Dark Pad*, *Tenor Sax Growl DNC*).
- A **Keyboard Set** is a complete performance snapshot for your hands:
  - **Right Hand (Upper)**: Up to **3 layered Sounds** (`UP1`, `UP2`, `UP3`)—for example, *Concert Grand* (`UP1` playing) + *Lush Strings* (`UP2` muted until you unmute it on screen).
  - **Left Hand (Lower)**: **1 Sound** (`LOW`, e.g., *Movie Strings* or *Acoustic Bass*) played below the Split Point.
  - Also remembers effects, Upper Octave, Ensemble harmony, and an optional local Split Point.

## 2. How to Change Voices on Stage (4 Ways, Fastest First)

### Way A — Bottom-Right `KEYBOARD SET LIBRARY` Buttons (Fastest General Voices)
Look at the **9 tall buttons on the bottom-right** (`PIANO & EP`, `ORGAN`, `GUITAR`, `STRINGS`, `BRASS`, `TRUMPET`, `SAX&WOOD.`, `SYNTH`, `ETHNIC`):
1. **CRITICAL GIG CHECK**: Look at the **`SET LIST`** button immediately to the left of `PIANO & EP`:
   - **LED OFF** = **Voice Library Mode** (pressing `ORGAN` gives you an Organ, pressing `BRASS` gives you Brass).
   - **LED ON (Red)** = **SongBook Set List Mode** (those 9 buttons now act as numbered song presets `1–9` and will change your Style and Tempo too!). **Turn `SET LIST` OFF** if you just want instrument categories.
2. Press any category button (e.g. `PIANO & EP` or `SAX&WOOD.`) to load the first voice in that family and open the **Keyboard Set Library (`298` manual Sets)** browser on the TouchView screen. Press the same button repeatedly to step through sets (e.g. all `40` Organ sets or `48` Piano sets).
3. Tap any slot in the `2×4` TouchView grid (or use **`P1–P6`** bank pills at the bottom). Toggle **`HOLD`** in the top-right of the TouchView bar to keep the browser open while auditioning, or turn **`HOLD` OFF** so tapping an item auto-closes back to the Main Page; press **`EXIT`** at any time to close.

### Way B — `KEYBOARD SET 1 – 4` Directly Under the Screen (Matched to Your Style)
Every Style on the Pa700 (`379` Factory Styles across `12` genres) comes with **4 curated Keyboard Sets** tailored to that groove, displayed in the 4 boxes along the bottom of the Main screen directly above physical buttons **`1`, `2`, `3`, `4`**:
- Press **`1`** for the sparse/verse voice, **`2`** or **`3`** for solo/chorus voices, **`4`** for the big lead/climax voice.
- **Pro Tip — `STYLE TO KBD SET` (round button left of `STYLE SELECT`)**:
  - **LED Off**: Changing Styles or Variations *never* changes your voice (safest if you want to stay on Piano all song!).
  - **LED Solid Red**: Picking a new Style automatically loads its `Keyboard Set 1`.
  - **LED Blinking Red**: Pressing `VARIATION 1–4` *automatically* switches your right-hand voice to `Keyboard Set 1–4` at the same time!

### Way C — `"Panic Home" Piano`: The `MY SETTING` Button
Lost in menus or stuck on a weird synth patch?
- Press **`MY SETTING`** (white-boxed label on the bottom row, left of `SONG BOOK`). It immediately restores your default startup Piano setup (`Concert Grand` on `UP1`). *(Long-press `MY SETTING` to save your current voices as your default!)*

### Way D — Layering or Swapping Individual Sounds (`1,845` Factory + Legacy + GM/XG + `DNC`)
On the right half of the **Style Play Main Page** (press `STYLE PLAY` then `EXIT` to guarantee you're there), you see 4 stacked rows (`UP1`, `UP2`, `UP3`, `LOW`):
- **Layer a 2nd voice instantly**: Tap the **`MUTE / PLAY`** button on `UP2` or `UP3`—now Piano + Strings play together!
- **Swap a single layer's Sound**: Tap the Sound name once to highlight the row, then **tap it a second time** (or press the `SOUND` Mode button) to open **Sound Select** with **all 1,845 manual Sounds** (`1,727 melodic Sounds + 118 Drum Kits`):
  - Switch between **`Factory`**, **`Legacy`**, and **`GM/XG`** tabs at the top, pick a category column on the left/right (`Piano`, `E. Piano`, `Mallet & Bell`, `Organ`, `Guitar`, `Strings & Vocal`, `Brass`, `Sax`, `Woodwind`, `Synth`, `Ethnic`, `Bass`, `Drum & SFX`), and step with `P1–P9` or the **`VALUE`** dial.
  - Sounds marked **`DNC`** (Defined Nuance Control — 52 manual sounds like `Tenor Sax DN1`) respond live to **`SW 1`**, **`SW 2`**, and Joystick **`Y+ / Y-`** (their active articulation strip appears in Sound Select and in the amber `DNC` pill in the Audio HUD).
- **Octave & Key Shifts**: Use **`UPPER OCTAVE - / +`** (shifts your right-hand pitch up/down octaves) and **`TRANSPOSE ♭ / ♯`** (transposes the entire keyboard & band in semitones; press both `-` and `+` together to reset to `0`).

## 3. Changing the Split Point & Full-Keyboard Piano Mode

### Toggle Split vs. Full 61-Key Piano
Look at the **`SPLIT`** button (right panel, between `METRONOME` and `ENSEMBLE`):
- **`SPLIT` LED ON**: Keyboard is divided at the Split Point (default **`C4` / Middle C**). Left hand (`< C4`) feeds chords to the Arranger + plays `LOW`; right hand (`>= C4`) plays `UP1–UP3`.
- **`SPLIT` LED OFF (Full Mode)**: `LOW` is turned off and **`UP1–UP3` span all 61 keys** like an acoustic piano! *(Tip: If `ACCOMP.` is still ON in Full Mode, playing 3+ notes anywhere still triggers chord recognition.)*

### Move the Split Position in 1 Second (No Menu Diving!)
1. **Press and HOLD the `SPLIT` button** (~1 second) until the green-bordered **`Split Point`** box pops up on screen (`Press a key to set`).
2. **Press the key on the keyboard** that you want to be the **lowest note of your right hand (Upper)** (for example, `F3` to give your right hand more room, or `E4` if your left hand plays higher chord voicings). Done!
- *Alternative on screen*: On the Main Page, tap the **`Split`** tab along the very bottom of the TouchView display, tap the `Set Point` box, and spin the **`VALUE` dial**.

## 4. Running the Auto-Arranger Live (Step-by-Step Song Workflow)

### Step 1: Pre-Song Checklist (5 Seconds Before Counting Off)
1. Press **`STYLE PLAY`** (top-left Mode section), then **`STYLE SELECT`** (or tap the Style box on screen) to browse all **379 Factory Styles** across 12 genre tabs (`Pop` through `World`) and `P1–P5` pages; press **`EXIT`**. *(Need an exact groove fast? Press **`🔍 SEARCH`** to filter across all 379 Styles, 298 Kbd Sets, 1,845 Sounds, and 415 Pads live!)*
2. Adjust speed with **`TEMPO - / +`** (or hold `SHIFT` + spin **`VALUE`** for fast jumps; press **`LOCK`** 🔒 if you don't want switching Styles to change your BPM).
3. Check the 3 left-hand LEDs (middle-left row):
   - **`SPLIT`** = **ON** (so left hand stays out of your melody range)
   - **`ACCOMP.`** = **ON** (turns on Bass + 5 band tracks; if OFF, you only hear Drums & Percussion!)
   - **`MEMORY`** = **ON** (crucial! Keeps the band playing your chord & bassline even when you lift your left hand to prepare the next chord)
4. Check **`AUTO FILL`** (round button between `VARIATION 4` and `BREAK`) = **ON** (plays realistic 1-bar drum/band fills automatically whenever you change Variations).

### Step 2: Arm the Start (Hands-Free)
1. Press **`VARIATION 1`** (sparsest arrangement—great for Verse 1). Its red LED lights solid.
2. *(Optional Intro)* Press one of the **`INTRO`** buttons on the bottom-left:
   - **`INTRO 1`**: Full multi-chord musical intro with a **prerecorded chord progression & melody** (play your song's key chord—e.g. G Major—and let it run!).
   - **`INTRO 2`**: Plays an intro riff **staying strictly on the chord(s) you play**.
   - **`INTRO 3 / COUNT IN`**: Just a **1-measure drum count-in** before dropping straight into your Variation.
   *(Notice that once you press an `INTRO`, the `VARIATION 1` LED starts **blinking**—meaning Variation 1 is "booked" to start right after the Intro finishes!)*
3. Press **`SYNCHRO START`** (round button just left of the big red `START/STOP` button).
4. **Play your first left-hand chord** (below the Split Point)—the entire band launches on beat 1!
   - *(Note: On the red `START/STOP` button, the LED flashes **RED on Beat 1** and **GREEN on Beats 2, 3, 4** so you always see the downbeat out of the corner of your eye!)*

### Step 3: Steer Energy Through the Song
- **Build intensity**: **`VARIATION 1`** (sparse Verse) $\rightarrow$ **`VARIATION 2`** (Verse 2 / Pre-Chorus) $\rightarrow$ **`VARIATION 3`** (Chorus) $\rightarrow$ **`VARIATION 4`** (Big Final Chorus / Solo).
  - With `AUTO FILL` ON, press the next Variation button during the last bar of a phrase; it plays a 1-bar fill and lands cleanly on the new Variation at Bar 1.
  - Pressing the *currently active* Variation button again triggers a Fill without changing Variations!
- **Dramatic Stop-Time**: Press **`BREAK`** near the end of a bar for a **1-measure band suspension/hit**, automatically resuming your Variation on the next bar.
- **Loop any Intro/Break/Ending**: Double-press `INTRO`, `BREAK`, or `ENDING` (its LED blinks) to keep cycling that section until you press a Variation!
- **Balance Band vs. Solo Volume**: Turn the **`BALANCE`** knob (top-left, right of `MASTER VOLUME`) clockwise toward `KBD` if the backing band is drowning out your right hand!

### Step 4: Nail the Ending
Press one of the **`ENDING`** buttons (or `FADE IN/OUT`)—the Arranger will play the ending and **stop automatically**:
- **`ENDING 1`**: Full **prerecorded cadential chord turnaround & melody** (waits until the current measure finishes).
- **`ENDING 2`**: Ending riff **holding your recognized chord** (waits until the current measure finishes).
- **`ENDING 3`**: **Immediate 2-measure punch ending**—starts *right away* without waiting for the end of the bar (lifesaver if a singer cuts a song short!).
- **`FADE IN/OUT`** (round button right of `START/STOP`): Smoothly fades the whole band down to silence and stops (or starts from silence if pressed while stopped).

## 5. Triggering & Assigning Pads (`415` Factory Pads) & SoundFont Selector

- **Trigger Pads 1–4**: Press **`PAD 1–4`** (lower-left above the Arranger strip) to fire One-Shot percussion/FX hits (`HIT`) or tempo-synced rhythmic loops (`SEQ`). Press **`STOP`** immediately right of `PAD 4` to silence all active Pads.
- **Assign Any of the 415 Manual Pads**: Double-click **`PAD 1–4`** (or open the **`Pad`** bottom tab on TouchView and click **`Pick`**) to open **Pad Select** across all `20` `HIT` & `SEQ` banks.
- **SoundFont Audio Engine (Top HUD)**: Switch between **`FluidR3 GM (Stream)`**, **`GeneralUser GS (.sf3/.sf2)`**, **`MuseScore General (.sf3)`**, **`FreePats GM (.sf2 Stack via + .sf2)`**, and **`Procedural WebAudio (Offline)`**. Streamed instrument banks download on demand and cache in memory; keys released before a patch finishes downloading suppress delayed ghost notes.
